Rider's Wells

The Rider's Wells keep horses rested, watered, and ready for war. It reduces cavalry training time in this village by 1% per level. At level 10, all Sentinels consume 1 less food per hour. At level 15, all Sun Riders consume 1 less food per hour. At level 20, all Crimson Lancers consume 1 less food per hour.

Rider's Wells below level 10

Levels 1–9 shorten cavalry training by 1% per level; the upkeep-reduction series is zero at those levels.

Limits: Sentinel upkeep reduction starts at level 10, Sun Rider at 15 and Crimson Lancer at 20 according to the source description. Do not read aggregate values 1/2/3 as a reduction for every cavalry unit.
