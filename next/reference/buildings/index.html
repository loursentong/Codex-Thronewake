REFERENCE

Academy

New unit types can be researched in the Academy. By increasing its level, you can order the research of better units.

↗REFERENCE

Barracks

The barracks allow you to train infantry units. The higher its level, the faster the troops are trained.

↗REFERENCE

Rally Point

Your village's troops gather at the Rally Point. From here you can send them out to settle, conquer, attack, raid, scout, or reinforce other villages. The level of the Rally Point also directly determines the threshold for how many incoming troops are visible to you.

↗REFERENCE

Town Hall

The village's master builders live in the town hall. The higher its level the faster your master builders complete the construction of buildings and fields.

↗REFERENCE

Workshop

The workshop allows you to build and upgrade siege engines. The higher its level, the faster the siege engines are built.
