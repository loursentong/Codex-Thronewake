Buildings

Follow the facts, their prerequisites and their sources.

Search all entries and filter by type, faction or research tier ↗

Building

Academy

New unit types can be researched in the Academy. By increasing its level, you can order the research of better units.

↗Building

Ancient Monument

A colossal relic of the old world, raised by The Ancients to defy time itself. Whoever completes it first claims dominion over the realm. As long as there is an Ancient Monument in the village, it cannot be conquered.

↗Building

Bannerfield

The Bannerfield prepares troops for long-distance journeys. It increases troop speed for the part of a journey beyond 20 fields, but does not affect merchants.

↗Building

Barracks

The barracks allow you to train infantry units. The higher its level, the faster the troops are trained.

↗Building

Brickyard

The brickyard fires clay into bricks, increasing the efficiency of your stone production. It increases the production of stone 5% per level.

↗Building

Butcher

The butcher prepares livestock from your grazing fields, increasing the efficiency of food production. It increases food production by 5% per level.

↗Building

Embassy

The Embassy is the diplomatic center of your villages. It allows you to create and join alliances.

↗Building

Expedition Camp

From the Expedition Camp, troops are sent beyond the village borders to secure nearby Wilder Sites. At levels 5, 10, and 15 it unlocks one claim slot, up to three Wilder Sites total.

↗Building

Farm

The farm provides food to support your growing population. Upgrade your grazing fields within the farm here to increase food production. This building is mandatory in your village.

↗Building

Festival Grounds

The Festival Grounds are where your people gather to honor victories, forge traditions, and strengthen the spirit of the realm. Start a celebration to gain Culture Points immediately.

↗Building

Forge

The forge refines metal, increasing the efficiency of your metal production. It increases the production of metal 5% per level.

↗Building

Foundry

The foundry produces metal. Upgrade your iron mines here to increase the metal production. This building is mandatory in your village.

↗Building

Granary

The granary stores food. Higher levels increase the storage capacity for food.

↗Building

Great Barracks

The Great Barracks allow you to train infantry units at triple the resource cost. Cannot be built in the capital village. The higher its level, the faster the troops are trained.

↗Building

Great Granary

A vast granary reserved for Ancient villages. It stores enough food to sustain a massive war effort.

↗Building

Great Stable

The Great Stable allows you to train cavalry units at triple the resource cost. Cannot be built in the capital village. The higher its level, the faster the troops are trained.

↗Building

Great Warehouse

A massive warehouse reserved for Ancient villages. It stores lumber, clay, and iron far beyond the limits of a normal warehouse.

↗Building

Herbalist

The Herbalist tends to wounded troops after battle, preparing remedies and treatments that allow up to 40% of fallen units to recover over time. Siege, leader and settler units cannot be healed.

↗Building

Lumberyard

The lumberyard produces lumber. Upgrade your woodcutters here to increase the lumber production. This building is mandatory in your village.

↗Building

Marketplace

The marketplace allows you to trade resources with other players. The higher its level, the more merchants you have.

↗Building

Palace

The Palace is the seat of power of your empire. As long as there is a Palace in the village, it cannot be conquered. At level 10, 15, and 20 you unlock an expansion slot, allowing you to train settlers or leaders. With settlers you can found new villages, and with leaders you can conquer other villages. A Palace also allows you to make this village your capital.

↗Building

Quarry

The quarry produces stone. Upgrade your clay pits within the quarry here to increase the stone production. This building is mandatory in your village.

↗Building

Rally Point

Your village's troops gather at the Rally Point. From here you can send them out to settle, conquer, attack, raid, scout, or reinforce other villages. The level of the Rally Point also directly determines the threshold for how many incoming troops are visible to you.

↗Building

Residence

The residence is a small palace that the king lives in while he visits. As long as there is a Residence in the village, it can never be captured. At level 10 and 20 you unlock an expansion slot, allowing you to train settlers or leaders. With settlers you can found new villages, and with leaders you can conquer other villages.

↗Building

Rider's Wells

The Rider's Wells keep horses rested, watered, and ready for war. It reduces cavalry training time in this village by 1% per level. At level 10, all Sentinels consume 1 less food per hour. At level 15, all Sun Riders consume 1 less food per hour. At level 20, all Crimson Lancers consume 1 less food per hour.

↗Building

Sawmill

The sawmill processes lumber, increasing the efficiency of your lumber production. It increases the production of lumber 5% per level.

↗Building

Shelter

The Shelter hides a portion of your village resources from enemy attacks and raids. Hidden resources cannot be looted.

↗Building

Smithy

Troops' armors and weapons are enhanced here. Each village has separate upgrades. Once a unit is upgraded, the effect applies to current and future units of that type.

↗Building

Smokehouse

The smokehouse preserves cuts from the butcher, increasing the efficiency of food production. It increases food production by 5% per level.

↗Building

Stable

The stable allows you to train cavalry units. The higher its level, the faster the troops are trained.

↗Building

Stonemason's Lodge

The Stonemason is an expert in cutting stone. The higher the level of the Stonemason's Lodge, the greater the stability of this village's buildings. This makes them more resistant to attacks from rams and catapults. The effect only applies to the Capital village.

↗Building

Stormbrew Works

The Stormbrew Works fuels warriors with roaring courage and reckless fury. Under its influence, armies strike harder, but discipline gives way to chaos. While an active Stormbrew Celebration is running in your capital at battle time, attacks from your villages gain +1% attack per level. However, catapult targets are forced to random hits, and leader units reduce loyalty by only 50% of their normal amount.

↗Building

Thornsnare Grove

The Thornsnare Grove hides roots, snares, and sharpened traps beneath the village's outer paths, waiting for attacking troops to stumble into its grasp. Captured troops keep consuming food from their origin village. Trapping enemy units permanently reduces the travel time of reinforcements sent from this village by up to 20%.

↗Building

Town Hall

The village's master builders live in the town hall. The higher its level the faster your master builders complete the construction of buildings and fields.

↗Building

Trade Office

In the Trade Office you can upgrade your merchants' carts and improve them with better horses. The further you upgrade this building the more resources your merchants will be able to carry.

↗Building

Treasury

Behind guarded doors and sealed chambers, the Treasury protects the rare artefacts that can shape the fate of a world. An empty Treasury is needed to claim an artefact for this village: level 10 for small artefacts and Ancient Monument plans, level 15 for large artefacts, and level 20 for unique artefacts.

↗Building

Warehouse

The warehouse stores lumber, stone and metal. Higher levels increase the storage capacity per resource individually.

↗Building

Watch Tower

Provides a defense bonus for your troops. The higher the level, the more effective your units are against enemy attacks.

↗Building

Workshop

The workshop allows you to build and upgrade siege engines. The higher its level, the faster the siege engines are built.
