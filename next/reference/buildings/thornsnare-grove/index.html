THRONEWAKE — REFERENCE

Thornsnare Grove

The Thornsnare Grove hides roots, snares, and sharpened traps beneath the village's outer paths, waiting for attacking troops to stumble into its grasp. Captured troops keep consuming food from their origin village. Trapping enemy units permanently reduces the travel time of reinforcements sent from this village by up to 20%.
