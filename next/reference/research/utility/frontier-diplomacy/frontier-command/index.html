These descriptions are English editorial translations of certified client text. The research name is sourced from the English interface. Current qualifications below remain applicable.

Expedition Camp plus Frontier Command slots

Lisan confirms four Wilder Site slots for the stated combination of three Expedition Camp slots and Frontier Command.

Lisan al-Gaib of Throne; Discord reply shown under 17 September 2026 in screenshots supplied by Jeremy. Original experience date, world and speed not provided. See L18_COMMUNITY.
