Rank effects

Each entry is the total effect at that rank, not the sum of preceding ranks.

RANK 1

Attacks and raids without siege units take 2% less time to reach their target, only if this village attacked or raided that target in the last 24 hours and lost fewer than half its units.

RANK 2

Attacks and raids without siege units take 4% less time to reach their target, only if this village attacked or raided that target in the last 24 hours and lost fewer than half its units.

RANK 3

Attacks and raids without siege units take 6% less time to reach their target, only if this village attacked or raided that target in the last 24 hours and lost fewer than half its units.

These descriptions are English editorial translations of certified client text. The research name is sourced from the English interface. Current qualifications below remain applicable.

A research's position in a branch does not prove its prerequisites. Only explicitly documented prerequisite observations are shown; no complete unlock tree or higher-rank cost curve is inferred.
