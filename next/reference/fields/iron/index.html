Level-22 capital fields and economic feasibility

Former-capital resource fields

Lisan reports that former-capital fields above the applicable cap are reduced to level13 in a City or level10 in an ordinary village.

Lisan, Discord follow-up displayed 18 September at16:49: if also a City level13, if normal village10; explicitly qualifies fields previously13+. World/speed/event date absent.

Limits: Accepted player testimony about reduction ceilings. Lower fields are not upgraded. Exact timing of the reduction remains unanswered; no capital move performed by Codex.
